## Strategic Assessment

**Opportunity level**: **VERY HIGH**

**Rationale**:
1. Primary keyword KD = 0 (zero competition difficulty)
2. Top result (Andreani) is a thin form page with no editorial content — easy to outclass
3. We already rank #4 on the broader "calcolo compenso avvocato penale" with our civil-only page — a dedicated penale page should jump to top 3 quickly
4. SERP is dominated by Andreani iframe embeds (studiodipietro.it, avvocatoflorio.com) — these are duplicates of the same UI, no editorial moat
5. No competitor has: full editorial content + calculator + schema markup + legal source links — we will be the only one
6. AI Overview is already pulling from third-party sources (studiolegalevescia, miolegale, penalistimilano) — strong GEO opportunity

**Differentiation angles**:
1. **Single integrated calculator covering all 13 competenze + 4 fasi** (Andreani has the form, but no editorial; tables sites have tables but no tool)
2. **Editorial depth**: legal commentary on each competenza, fase, and the relationship with civil compenso (cross-link to /calcolo-compenso, /compenso-stragiudiziale)
3. **Full normativa stack**: DM 55/2014 (art. 1, 2, 12, 15, 27) + DM 147/2022 + L. 247/2012 + L. 217/2024 (recent gratuito patrocinio reforms)
4. **Gratuito patrocinio integration**: dedicated section with 2026 reddito limits
5. **AI Overview optimization**: passage-level Q&A blocks aligned with PAA questions

**Risks**:
- AI Overview already exists for "quanto costa avvocato penale" — must target citability via TL;DR boxes
- Andreani has brand authority (4,6 rating, 715 reviews) — we need trust signals (E-E-A-T)
- Tables data must match D.M. 147/2022 exactly — must verify against Normattiva
