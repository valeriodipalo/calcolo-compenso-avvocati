## Strategic Assessment

**Opportunity level: HIGH.**

- **Calculator-driven SERP**: 5 of top 10 are calculator tools (avvocatoandreani, lex365 ×2, professionegiustizia, dirittopratico). Building one is table stakes.
- **Two regimes split across two pages**: every major competitor (avvocatoandreani, professionegiustizia, dirittopratico) ships **two separate calculators** — one for the vecchio rito (183/190) and one for Cartabia (171-ter/189). **Our differentiation: one unified tool that asks the procedure-start date and routes automatically.**
- **PAA dominated by AI-Overview seeds**: the SERP shows multiple AIO expanded elements — strong opportunity for citability if H2s are framed as questions and answers are passage-friendly.
- **Schema/rich result gap**: only avvocatoandreani has rating snippet, no competitor implements WebApplication/HowTo schemas comprehensively.
- **Existing cluster integration**: pages `calcolo-termini-processuali`, `calcolo-termini-impugnazione`, `calcolo-giorni-tra-date`, `opposizione-decreto-ingiuntivo` already LIVE — internal-link advantage for any new termini page.

**Differentiation angles:**
1. **Unified calculator** — single input flow with auto-detection of regime (pre-Cartabia 183/190 vs post-Cartabia 171-ter/189/275-bis) based on procedure-start date
2. **All 5 deadline scenarios in one tool**: (a) memorie 183 c. 6 nn. 1-2-3 a partire da udienza, (b) comparse 190 a partire da rimessione, (c) memorie integrative 171-ter a ritroso da prima udienza, (d) note precisazione + comparse + repliche 189 a ritroso da udienza collegiale, (e) note + comparse 275-bis discussione orale
3. **Sabato/festivi logic transparent** — show both modalità (prudenziale vs Cass. 13201/2006) with toggle
4. **Sospensione feriale and emergenza COVID** properly handled
5. **Tabella di confronto vecchio/nuovo rito** — high-value content nobody else has done well
6. **Schema markup full stack**: BreadcrumbList (auto), Article, WebApplication, Dataset (deadline table)
7. **Rimessione in termini and tardività section** — addresses PAA gaps

**Risks:**
- AI Overview present on the SERP → losing CTR; mitigation: passage-level structure with explicit answers
- avvocatoandreani's incumbency is strong (1.613 reviews) — we must out-detail
- Topic is heavily abrogated content (183/190) — must clearly mark sunset date 28/02/2023 with positive framing of new regime
