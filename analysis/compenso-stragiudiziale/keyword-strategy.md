## Strategic Assessment

**Opportunity level: HIGH**

The aggregated cluster volume across primary+secondary targets exceeds 14,000/month, with very low KD across the board (competition: 0 on all major terms). The dominant competitor (Andreani) has 4.6/745 reviews and a working calculator — they're the only real wall.

### Differentiation Angles

1. **DM 147/2022 freshness** — Andreani's page targets "DM 55/2014" in title; D.M. 147/2022 updated Table 25 rates upward (~+8-10%). Our title leads with the current decree.
2. **Both heads in one page** — competitors split "parametri" (technical/CNF audience) vs "compenso/tariffa" (user audience). One page targeting both intents owns the whole cluster.
3. **Interactive calculator with breakdown** — most top-10 results have static tables only. Andreani has a calculator but minimal explanation. Our calculator + complete editorial wins both UX and content depth.
4. **Cross-cluster linking** — our existing `calcolo-compenso`, `calcolo-fattura-avvocato` pages can deep-link to this page (and vice versa) reinforcing the compensi cluster topic authority.
5. **PAA coverage on "chi paga"** — Cass. 24481/2020 ruling on spese legali stragiudiziali as danno emergente is a high-value PAA-driven section nobody covers in depth.
6. **Practical contexts not in SERP**: transazione stragiudiziale, parere stragiudiziale, redazione contratto, fase di mediazione — each is a Google related search with no dedicated competitor section.

### Risks

- AI Overview is replacing some PAA expansions — must structure content for LLM extraction (passage-level citability, clear definitions, table-with-source format).
- Andreani has years of accumulated user-rating snippet (4.6/745) — direct displacement at #1 takes time even with better content. Realistic 90-day target: top 3.
- Our existing `calcolo-compenso` page already ranks #9 for one variant — must add canonical/internal-link strategy to prevent self-cannibalization (the new page targets stragiudiziale specifically; the existing one targets judicial fees).
