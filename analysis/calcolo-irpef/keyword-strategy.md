## Strategic Assessment

### Opportunity Level: **HIGH**

**Why we can win**:
- KD 17 is moderate — primary keyword is winnable for a focused, deep page
- The #1 (avvocatoandreani.it) is our direct competitor template — beating its UX, content depth, and schema is the explicit project goal
- The 2026 reform (35% → 33%) is recent and rapidly changing — our page can be the most up-to-date in the SERP
- AI Overview is heavily citing institutional sources — strong content with verifiable citations to Agenzia Entrate / Normattiva can earn citations
- Existing fiscale cluster (TFR, Scorporo IVA, Ravvedimento Operoso) creates topical authority

### Differentiation Angles
1. **Aliquote 2026 already integrated** (most competitors still show 2025) — explicit "Aggiornato Legge di Bilancio 2026"
2. **Complete year selector**: 2024 / 2025 / 2026 toggle in the calculator (no competitor offers this)
3. **Three calculation modes**: lordo→netto IRPEF, simulazione progressiva per scaglioni, calcolo aliquota media e marginale
4. **Worked examples for typical incomes** (15k, 25k, 35k, 50k, 75k, 100k) with side-by-side 2025 vs 2026 savings
5. **Addizionali regionali e comunali** mentioned and linked (most competitors skip this critical detail)
6. **Detrazioni base** (lavoro dipendente, pensione, autonomi) integrated in the calculator's "netto" mode
7. **No tax area** explicitly explained (rising query)
8. **Schema markup**: BreadcrumbList + Article + WebApplication (Dataset for scaglioni table). No FAQ schema (Google restriction).
9. **Internal linking**: cross-link to TFR (uses IRPEF for tassazione separata), Scorporo IVA, Ravvedimento Operoso (IRPEF unpaid), Calcolo Fattura Avvocato (cassa, IVA)

### Risks
- **AI Overview** dominates fold for "scaglioni irpef 2026" — must structure content for citation (passages, headers, lists)
- **Agenzia Entrate** has authoritative position #4 — we can't match official authority, but can offer the calculation engine they don't
- **Topic decay** for "calcolo irpef 2025" — short-lived. Year-agnostic primary keyword is the right target.
