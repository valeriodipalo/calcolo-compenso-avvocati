## Strategic Framing

The cluster spans **two distinct macro-domains** (Fiscale + Lavoro) with three tools serving very different jobs:
- Scorporo IVA → emission/reception of invoices
- TFR → end-of-employment liquidation
- Ravvedimento Operoso → post-violation tax regularization

**No single high-volume keyword unites all three.** The DFS data confirms this: keywords spanning all three (e.g., "fiscalità professionisti", "calcoli fiscali", "guida fiscale e lavoro") have <50 SV. High-volume informational keywords like "scadenze fiscali 2026" (1,000 SV) are dominated by monthly scadenzario pages from Agenzia Entrate and Fisco7.it — a static pillar can't compete.

**Therefore the pillar's primary purpose is INTERNAL NAVIGATION**, not capturing a single hero keyword. The strategy mirrors the existing `interessi-e-rivalutazione` pillar: a synthetic compound primary that captures cumulative orientation traffic across multiple informational long-tails.

## Strategic Assessment

- **Opportunity**: **Medium**. The cluster's broadness limits a single-keyword hero, but the cumulative long-tail informational traffic (~3,000+ monthly) is meaningful. Realistically the pillar will capture mid-low monthly clicks with a stable ~5-12 informational queries.
- **Differentiation**: We are the only pillar combining (a) deep editorial orientation, (b) 3 working interactive calculators, and (c) a unifying "fiscale e lavoro" framing for professionals. Commercialisti guides have content but no tools; AdE has authority but no orientation.
- **Risks**:
  - SERP fragmentation across 10+ different long-tails (no single ranking signal compounds)
  - Agenzia Entrate dominates the gov-authority SERP for professionali
  - "Scadenze 2026" SERP is operational/monthly — we cannot compete head-on, only via section-level mentions
- **Mitigations**:
  - Position the page primarily as INTERNAL HUB (funnel to tools), with SEO traffic as secondary value
  - Use the "differenze" comparison table — the unique angle no commercialista or scadenzario page has
  - Update sitemap `lastModified` regularly to remain fresh (we're already on 2026-05-05)
